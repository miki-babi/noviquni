## Never ships (V1)

**Hard no:** anything that isn’t academic study resources.

- Campus Survival OS
- Registration / ID / dorm / costs / section logistics
- Career paths / internship product
- Opportunity marketplace
- Portfolio builder
- National “all Ethiopia PDFs” factory
- Assignment / homework mill
- Feature-checklist arms race for its own sake
- SEO as the product (site = CTA only)

**Why:** Commodity volume loses to Qeleme; logistics ≠ our product; career is a later wedge.

**Revisit only after:** beachhead density + paid signal.

**Honesty rule:** If ads mention opportunities, use manual drops only — don’t ship as product in V1.
